# **Generic Book Bible — Modular Template (Fits Any Book Type)**

## **הרעיון המרכזי**

בייבל אחד עם מבנה קבוע לכל הספרים לא יעבוד — ספר ילדים לא צריך "world-building rules" וספר טכני לא צריך "character arc". הפתרון: **גרעין אוניברסלי** (שדות שרלוונטיים לכל ספר, בלי יוצא מן הכלל) \+ **מודול ספציפי לסוג הספר** שמצרפים לפי הצורך. כשמתחילים ספר חדש: ממלאים את הגרעין \+ בוחרים/ממלאים מודול אחד (או משלבים שניים, למשל ספר ילדים לימודי \= Children's \+ Instructional).

בסוף המסמך יש גם הערה חשובה: **סוג הספר משנה גם את תפקידי ה-AI בפייפליין עצמו** (למשל ספר טכני צריך "בודק דיוק עובדתי" במקום "עורך עלילה").

## **חלק א׳: הגרעין האוניברסלי (חובה בכל ספר)**

BOOK METADATA:  
  title:   
  working\_subtitle:   
  book\_type: \[fiction-novel / children's / technical-instructional / self-help-lifestyle / memoir / business / other\]  
  target\_audience: \# age range, expertise level, or reader profile  
  language: English (default)   
  target\_total\_word\_count: \# range, e.g. 60,000-75,000  
  target\_chapter\_count:   
  target\_words\_per\_chapter: \# range

PURPOSE & PROMISE:  
  core\_promise: \# what does the reader walk away with? (a story experienced / a skill learned / a problem solved / a feeling evoked)  
  one\_sentence\_pitch:   
  why\_this\_book\_why\_now: \# optional but useful for nonfiction

VOICE & STYLE GUIDE:  
  point\_of\_view: \[first person / third person limited / third person omniscient / second person / N/A for nonfiction\]  
  tense: \[past / present\]  
  tone: \# e.g. warm and encouraging / dry and technical / whimsical / literary  
  formality\_level: \# casual conversational \-\> academic formal  
  sentence\_complexity: \# short & punchy vs. long & flowing; reading level if relevant (e.g. Flesch-Kincaid grade X)  
  regional\_spelling: \[American English / British English\]  
  things\_to\_avoid: \# clichés, taboo topics, specific words/phrases, humor style to avoid, etc.

STRUCTURE / OUTLINE:  
  overall\_arc: \# 2-4 sentences: how does the book move from beginning to end, regardless of genre   
  chapter\_by\_chapter\_outline: \# table: chapter \# | working title | purpose of this chapter | key content/beats | target word count  
  \# For fiction: beats \= plot events. For nonfiction: beats \= concepts/skills taught, in what order, and why that order.

TERMINOLOGY / GLOSSARY:  
  locked\_terms: \# proper nouns, invented terms, technical terms, spelling conventions that must stay consistent  
  \# e.g. "e-mail" vs "email", a fantasy world's place names, a fixed acronym expansion

CONSTRAINTS & NON-NEGOTIABLES:  
  must\_include:   
  must\_avoid: \# legal/sensitive topics, content ratings, brand mentions, etc.  
  accuracy\_requirements: \# does this book require fact-checking, technical review, cited sources?

DEFINITION OF DONE:  
  chapter\_level: \# checklist for "this chapter is finished" (ties to editor roles)  
  book\_level: \# checklist for "this book is finished"

This core block feeds directly into the shared context block used by the Writer/Editor pipeline discussed earlier.

## **חלק ב׳: מודולים ספציפיים לפי סוג ספר**

בחר מודול אחד (או שילוב) והוסף אותו לגרעין.

### **מודול 1 — Fiction / Novel**

CHARACTERS:  
  \- name:  
    role: \[protagonist / antagonist / supporting\]  
    external\_goal:   
    internal\_need: \# what they actually need to grow, often different from what they want  
    flaw/wound:   
    voice\_notes: \# speech patterns, vocabulary, verbal tics — critical for multi-POV consistency  
    arc: \# where they start emotionally/morally, where they end  
    relationships: \# key dynamics with other characters

WORLD / SETTING:  
  time\_period\_and\_place:   
  world\_rules: \# magic systems, technology level, social/political structure — anything that must stay internally consistent  
  sensory\_palette: \# recurring imagery/motifs tied to setting or theme

PLOT:  
  central\_conflict:   
  stakes: \# what happens if the protagonist fails  
  subplots: \# list, with which chapters they touch  
  themes: \# underlying ideas explored (not stated outright in the text)  
  chekhovs\_guns: \# elements planted early that must pay off later — tracked and updated in Story State as the book progresses

PACING MAP:  
  act\_structure: \# e.g. 3-act, 5-act, or custom; which chapters belong to which act  
  tension\_curve: \# rough shape of rising/falling tension across the book

### **מודול 2 — Children's Book**

AUDIENCE SPECIFICS:  
  age\_range: \# e.g. 4-7  
  reading\_level: \# picture book (read-aloud) / early reader / middle grade  
  vocabulary\_constraints: \# word list restrictions, syllable limits, no words above X reading level  
  sentence\_length\_limit:   
  repetition\_pattern: \# many children's books use rhythmic/repeated phrases — define the pattern if used

CONTENT SHAPE:  
  page\_count\_or\_spread\_count: \# picture books are often structured by spread, not chapter  
  words\_per\_page\_or\_spread: \# often very small, e.g. 0-40 words  
  illustration\_notes: \# what needs to be depicted per page/spread, even if Claude isn't generating the art — needed for pacing and page-turns  
  page\_turn\_moments: \# where a page-turn should create surprise/anticipation

STORY/LESSON:  
  central\_lesson\_or\_theme: \# most children's books teach something (kindness, courage, a fact) — state it explicitly  
  emotional\_arc: \# simple beginning-\>middle-\>end emotional journey  
  safety\_and\_sensitivity: \# tone must stay age-appropriate; no frightening content beyond the defined comfort threshold; parental/caregiver acceptability  
  recurring\_character\_or\_mascot: \# if series-based

### **מודול 3 — Technical / Instructional / How-To**

LEARNER PROFILE:  
  prerequisite\_knowledge: \# what the reader must already know  
  learning\_objectives: \# per chapter — what should the reader be able to DO after reading it (skills, not just facts)

CONTENT ACCURACY:  
  subject\_matter\_scope: \# exact boundaries of what's covered vs. explicitly out of scope  
  version\_or\_currency\_requirements: \# e.g. "covers Python 3.12", "current tax law as of 2026" — critical for technical accuracy and dating  
  sources\_of\_truth: \# official docs, standards, references the content must align with  
  fact\_checking\_required: \[yes/no — if yes, this changes the pipeline: needs a Technical Accuracy Reviewer role, not a Structural/Plot Editor\]

STRUCTURE CONVENTIONS:  
  chapter\_template: \# e.g. every chapter \= concept intro \-\> worked example \-\> common pitfalls \-\> exercises \-\> summary  
  code\_or\_diagram\_requirements: \# language/framework versions, formatting conventions, whether diagrams are needed and in what format  
  exercises\_or\_practice\_sections: \# format, difficulty progression  
  cross\_referencing: \# how chapters reference each other (e.g. "see Chapter 3")

TONE FOR INSTRUCTION:  
  assumed\_reader\_frustration\_points: \# where readers typically get confused — address proactively  
  example\_domain: \# consistent running example/case study used throughout, if any, for continuity

### **מודול 4 — Self-Help / Lifestyle / Personal Development Nonfiction**

CORE THESIS:  
  central\_argument: \# the book's one big idea  
  supporting\_pillars: \# 3-5 sub-arguments/frameworks that build the case, roughly one per section

EVIDENCE & CREDIBILITY:  
  evidence\_type: \# personal anecdote / research citations / case studies / expert interviews — and how rigorously it must be sourced  
  citation\_requirements: \# if referencing studies, what sourcing standard is required (this matters a lot for factual accuracy review)

READER TRANSFORMATION:  
  starting\_state: \# where the reader is emotionally/practically before reading  
  ending\_state: \# where they should be after  
  actionable\_takeaways: \# exercises, reflection questions, or action steps — per chapter or per section  
  case\_studies\_or\_stories: \# recurring format for illustrative anecdotes, and whether these are real (needs consent/anonymization notes), composite, or illustrative examples (must be labeled as such)

STRUCTURE CONVENTIONS:  
  chapter\_template: \# e.g. hook story \-\> concept \-\> evidence \-\> practical exercise \-\> chapter summary/key takeaways box  
  callout\_boxes: \# sidebars, quotes, checklists — define which recurring formats are used

## **חלק ג׳: איך זה משפיע על תפקידי ה-AI בפייפליין**

זו הנקודה החשובה — לא רק ה-Bible משתנה, אלא **ה"Structural Editor" צריך תפקיד שונה לגמרי** לפי סוג הספר:

| סוג ספר | תחליף ל-"Structural Editor" |
| ----- | ----- |
| רומן/פיקשן | עורך עלילה/דמויות/מבנה (כפי שכבר הגדרנו) |
| ספר ילדים | בודק גיל-התאמה \+ קצב הקראה \+ עקביות אורך משפט/אוצר מילים |
| טכני/הדרכה | בודק דיוק טכני/עובדתי \+ שלמות ה-learning objectives \+ סדר לוגי של תלות בין מושגים |
| איכות חיים/פיתוח אישי | בודק חוזק הטיעון \+ איכות הראיות/מקורות \+ שהתרגילים מעשיים וברי-ביצוע |

ה-**Language Editor** וה-**Proofreader** נשארים כמעט זהים בכל הסוגים (דקדוק זה דקדוק), אבל ה-style guide שהם עובדים לפיו (אוצר מילים, אורך משפט) נגזר מה-Bible הספציפי.
